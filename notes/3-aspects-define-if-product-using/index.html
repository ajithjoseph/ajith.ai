3 aspects that define if the product you are using or intending to use is good for you or your team:

  1. You absolutely LOVE the product you're using, every day.
  1. The MAKERS of this product are good to work with when there is a need to talk to them.
  1. You can use the product and INTEGRATE well with your other set of tools.

If all of the above checks out, you are using the right product for your use case and you can be proud that you are a wise person.
