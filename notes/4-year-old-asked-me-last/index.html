My 4 year old asked me last night

My 4 year old asked me last night,

"Daddy, does God hear me when I whisper?"

I just froze. Sat on the edge of her bed.

And said, "He hears every word, baby."

She closed her eyes and started praying. Not loud. Just a little girl talking to God like He was sitting right there. That's what prayer is supposed to feel like.

Not perfectionism. Just a conversation.

That moment is why I built ThyWord.

Because somewhere along the way, a lot of us forgot how to just… talk to God.

We made it complicated. We made it intimidating. We stopped doing it altogether.

ThyWord is a prayer companion that listens to your voice, meets you where you are, and connects your words to Scripture.

No scripts. No pressure. Just you and God.

My 4 year old doesn't need an app to pray.

But millions of adults do need permission to start again.

That's what we're building.
