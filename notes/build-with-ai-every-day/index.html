I build with AI every day.

I build with AI every day.

Voice models, language models, pipelines that move faster than I can think.

And the more I build, the clearer one thing becomes.

AI can do a lot. It still can't do the things that make us human.

It can write a sympathy note. It can't sit with you.

It can draft an apology. It can't actually be sorry.

It can simulate care. It can't choose to care.

The hard part of being human was never the words. It was the meaning behind them.

The real risk isn't that AI replaces us. It's that we forget what only we can do.

Build with it. Don't outsource yourself to it.
