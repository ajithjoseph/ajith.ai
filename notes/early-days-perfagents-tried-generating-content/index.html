In the early days of PerfAgents we tried generating content using ChatGPT.

How did it go? It was terrible. It looked so artificial. It looked like it was generated even with some edits.

I then delegated to my team to write content for PerfAgents. It was naive but the overall quality got slightly better. But after some time we glorified quantity over quality (after listening in to a few YouTube videos on that). Then what happened was obvious. The team went back to ChatGPT. This time I was not aware that we used ChatGPT but it became evident that content is not human written.

Finally, I took charge and started writing and ever since there is an immense number of views on these posts even though engagement is still low.

Every post is written not generated.

And that shows up!

So the point is, don't yet rely on AI for content. Especially when the content is king 👑! It's the "give and take" model that still works.
