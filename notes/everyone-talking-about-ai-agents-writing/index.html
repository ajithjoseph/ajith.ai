Everyone is talking about AI agents writing code.

Everyone is talking about AI agents writing code.

Nobody is talking about who watches the code after it ships.

Anthropic just launched Managed Agents, Routines, and a full desktop experience for Claude Code.

This is not incremental.

They are building an operating system for the entire software engineering loop.

AI agents are about to deploy more code, more often, with less human review than anything we have seen before.

And the humans who used to catch problems by reading diffs and reviewing PRs?

They will be outnumbered.

When a human writes bad code, another human usually catches it in review.

When an AI agent writes bad code and ships it at 2am through an automated pipeline, the only thing standing between you and an outage is your observability stack.

Observability is no longer a monitoring tool. It is the immune system of software.

The next era belongs to platforms that move from "here is a dashboard showing something broke" to "we detected the anomaly, identified the root cause, and rolled it back before your customers noticed."

Agents watching agents. Built natively on open standards like OpenTelemetry.

Transparent pricing. Autonomous operations.

I have spent almost two decades helping enterprises adopt complex infrastructure.

The pattern is always the same: the picks and shovels win.

In the AI agent era, observability is the picks and shovels.
