Notes · April 3, 2026

Friday is good.

Friday is good.

Not because the week is over.
Not because the weekend is here.

Friday is good because sometimes the worst day becomes the most important one.

Every founder knows this feeling.

The day your biggest customer churns. The day your co-founder says "we need to talk." The day your bank account hits a number that makes your stomach drop.

You don't call those days good while you're in them. You call them good later. When you realize that was the day everything actually changed.

Two thousand years ago, a Friday looked like complete failure. A movement was over. The leader was gone. His closest team abandoned the project.

Nobody standing there called it good.

But that Friday became the most consequential product launch in human history. Three days later, everything changed. And we still talk about it.

So if you're in your "Friday" right now, the fundraise fell through, the product broke, the deal died, just know this:

Some Fridays only look bad from the inside.

Have a blessed Good Friday. The story isn't over.

Repost if your worst day turned into your best chapter.
