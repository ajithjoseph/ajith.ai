I grew up watching my grandmother pray every single morning in Kerala.

No app, no fancy setup.

Just her voice, her faith, and Scripture she knew by heart.

When I moved to the US, my faith slowly became a Sunday thing.

I stopped opening my Bible during the week. Prayer felt rushed. Life just took over.

Building ThyWord brought me back. And with Easter out in 15 days, I wanted to share 6 things that have honestly changed how I walk with God:

  1. Pray out loud with SCRIPTURE. Don't just read it. Speak it. When you hear God's Word in your own voice, something shifts inside you.
  1. Take five minutes of SILENCE before your day starts. No phone. No podcast. Just sit with Him. That's where you'll hear what matters.
  1. FAST from the thing that controls you most. For me it was my phone. For you it might be something else. You'll know what it is.
  1. SERVE someone who can't return the favor. Buy a coffee. Mow a lawn. Show up for somebody who isn't expecting it.
  1. INVITE one person into your faith journey this week. Send a text. Make a call. We weren't built to walk this road alone.
  1. Go home and love your family well. Give them YOUR PRESENCE, not your leftovers.

The EASTER season is ideally the Spring season and is perfect for a RESET.

Start with even one of these.
