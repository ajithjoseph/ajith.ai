If Sebastian Siemiatkowski of Klarna thinks that his own job will get replaced by AI agents, there is still one (hidden) happy element about it. That is, the amount of time that he gets back to enjoy and work on other things he loves.

And for me that - as a busy parent of young little ones and as a Founder, is getting back my 8 hours of sleep, ultra running training and quality family time with my wonderful family that supports me to put in the extra time that I now spend on building an amazing product that businesses and people love.
