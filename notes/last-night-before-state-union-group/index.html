Last night, before the State of the Union, a group of leaders stood in a circle and prayed. Just a quiet moment with God before walking into the biggest room in the country.

That image stopped me.

I spent years building software for Fortune 500 companies. But as a father of four, the only time I felt close to God was Sunday morning. The other six days I was on my own.

That gap is why we built ThyWord. You speak what is on your heart. It finds the Scripture that speaks back.

We launched the app on February 18th from right here in Jacksonville. An amazing team poured their hearts into this. We did not do it alone.

Whether it is leaders praying before a national address or a tired parent whispering "God help me" before the day starts, those quiet moments are where faith actually lives.
