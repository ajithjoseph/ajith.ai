When intelligence is free, meaning is the scarce thing

34 startups raised over $200M this year.

Robot brains.

World models.

Chips.

Coding agents.

Nuclear plants to feed the data centers.

Every dollar is chasing the same thing.

Cheaper, faster intelligence.

Almost nobody is funding the other half.

What a person actually does with that intelligence once it costs nothing.

That is my bet.

When intelligence is free, meaning is the scarce thing.

So I build at the application layer, not the engine room.

ThyWord, my voice first prayer app, has 12,000 downloads and not one of those 34 is competing for the ground I am on.

Everyone is building the engine.

What we point it at is wide open.
