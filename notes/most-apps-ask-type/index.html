Notes · March 26, 2026

Most apps ask you to type.

Most apps ask you to type. We ask you to speak.

When we built ThyWord, we made a deliberate design choice: voice first, everything else second.

No keyboard. No scrolling through menus.

You open the app, tap one button, and start talking.

Why?

Because prayer isn't something you type. It's something you pour out.

And when someone is in a moment of need, the last thing they want is friction.

Behind that simple microphone button is a real time speech to Scripture matching engine.

You speak what's on your heart, and ThyWord listens and responds with the exact Bible verses that speak to your situation.

Building this taught me something important about product design: the best technology disappears.

The user shouldn't be thinking about the AI, the transcription, or the matching algorithm.

They should just feel heard.

That gold mic button?

It's the entire product. One tap. One conversation.

One moment between you and the Divine Presence, powered by technology you never have to think about.

We launched on Ash Wednesday and we're just getting started.
