Running is my prayer room.

Running is my prayer room.

No walls. No roof. Just road and raw honesty.

When my legs want to quit, my soul starts talking.

When my lungs are burning, my mind gets quiet.

When everything hurts, somehow everything makes sense.

I don't run to escape life. I run to feel it.

Do you run?
