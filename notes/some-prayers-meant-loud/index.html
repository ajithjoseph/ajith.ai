Some prayers are not meant to be loud.

Some prayers are not meant to be loud.

The ones whispered at 2am. The ones you say with your eyes closed in a crowded room. The ones too fragile to speak above a breath.

When we built ThyWord, we kept coming back to this. A prayer companion that only works when you raise your voice would miss the most honest moments of all.

So we tuned it for the quiet. You can whisper, barely move your lips, and ThyWord still catches your words and matches them to Scripture in real time.

This was not simple on the engineering side. Soft speech, low volume, breathy input. These are some of the hardest things to get right in voice. We spent real time on it, because the whisper is where so much of faith actually lives.

My grandmother prayed under her breath every morning. I never once heard the words clearly. But I always knew she was praying.

That is who we built this for.

Try it for yourself. Whisper a prayer and watch the Scripture come back to you.
