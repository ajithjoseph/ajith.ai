Wednesday reminder to myself

Wednesday reminder to myself:

I spent 14 years building things for other people's companies.

Good salary. Good title. Good life.

But every Sunday at church, I felt something stirring.
Not guilt. Not restlessness. A calling.

In 2020, I finally made the jump into entrepreneurship.

Built. Failed. Learned. Built again.

Had an exit in 2025.

Then I noticed something nobody was solving.

There are 6 days between Sundays.

And most Christians are praying alone. In silence. With no guidance.

So in 2026, I built a voice first prayer companion that listens when you pray and responds with Scripture.

We launched on Ash Wednesday.

And the response has been incredible.

Am I scared? Every single day.

Would I go back? Not a chance.

Wednesday is the middle of the week.

It's also the middle of the story.

And the middle is where faith gets tested the most.
