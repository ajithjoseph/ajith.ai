When battle tested playbooks are readily available, you skip the potholes.

And then, complex AI features won't be moon-shots anymore.

Those AI features will be modular, measurable, and will be living in production.

The only prerequisite for such an outcome is genuine AI-readiness.

Which means:

  1. Clean data
  2. A hyperscaler home (not necessary in some cases)
  3. One or two champions who want to level-up their craft.

The AI Readiness Checklist:

  1. Data Hygiene. For example: 5 years of data synced to Redshift. It gives your models solid ground truth.
  2. Cloud Footprint. For example: Everything already on AWS means zero procurement friction.
  3. Champion Users. For example: Pilot users who love tinkering. Continuous feedback loops. Exec sponsor(s) eager to lock margins. Budget to unblock decisions.

All combined, a great outcome is 100% guaranteed.
