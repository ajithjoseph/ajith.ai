To the user who sent this: You are the reason I am a second-time founder.

There is a lot of noise in the AI space right now, but I’ve always maintained that technology without mission is just code.

I built ThyWord to disrupt how we interact with faith. We stayed away from static text toward an immersive, voice-first conversational interface.

From a technical standpoint, creating that "encouragement" requires more than just an LLM wrapper.

It requires optimizing for low-latency, voice-first interactions where the system doesn’t just process words, but understands human intent in real-time.

When I architected this, the goal was to ensure the technology remained invisible so the impact could remain front and center.

Seeing the mission-market fit align so closely with the technical architecture is why I do what I do.

We are proving that you can scale "encouragement" through smart, visionary engineering.
